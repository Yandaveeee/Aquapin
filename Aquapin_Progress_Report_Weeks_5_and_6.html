G. Issues and Concerns

Problem Encountered: Some operational records required additional review to ensure that their presentation in the admin console was clear and consistent.

Impact on Project: Inconsistent presentation could make administrative monitoring and review more difficult.

Action Taken: The team reviewed the organization of the records and refined the interface for easier viewing.

Problem Encountered: Certain admin pages needed improvement when displayed on different screen sizes.

Impact on Project: Some functions could be less convenient to use on smaller displays.

Action Taken: The team adjusted the interface layout and improved its responsiveness.

H. Insights, Lessons Learned, and Adjustments

Insights Gained: Administrative tools are more useful when operational records are organized clearly and essential settings are easy to access.

Lessons Learned: Reviewing usability and responsiveness during development helps identify concerns before formal system testing begins.

Adjustments to the Project Plan: The team prioritized general interface refinement and record review before proceeding to automated verification, alerts, and testing.
